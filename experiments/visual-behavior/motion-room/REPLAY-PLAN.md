# Bounded follow-up after the V2 pilot

The V2 physical pilot retains exact sampled retinal arrays for both cameras at25Hz, their source/frame clocks, the initial image-conditioned baselines, and the original neural-flow outputs. This is enough to regenerate the full model response history without changing the physical trajectory. The replay would be a diagnostic, not a counterfactual body experiment: a different controller would change subsequent camera images.

Before comparing a new solver, regenerate the original neural-map-flow v1 outputs from those stored samples. Recreate the500-step fade from the first frame, compare the resulting baselines to the recorded signed baselines, and compare each regenerated flow result with its original value. Reject a replay if source/model identity or this numerical oracle differs. Do not quietly continue with a similar-looking response.

The aperture-aware solver in `shared/vision/readouts/neural-map-flow-v2.js` is a separate candidate. Its rank-one estimate measures only motion normal to a local pattern. For a declared horizontal-only camera-motion assumption, `normalVelocity / normalAxis.x` can estimate the horizontal rate when the normal has enough horizontal sensitivity; the discarded tangent is still unobserved. Merely using its minimum-norm `velocity.x` under slanted patterns would systematically shrink the requested turn. Neither interpretation automatically compensates actual body pitch/roll or translation.

Any such replay must report its geometric assumption and preserve the v1 failure. Confidence/gain choices can use only declared calibration data. Recorded physical pilot trajectories may expose why a candidate fails, but they cannot become fresh held-out acceptance evidence after being inspected. A new prospective renderer calibration and separately seeded body pilot must follow a successful diagnostic before considering the30-seed study. Keep the body gains unchanged during that comparison so improvements can be attributed to the changed readout.

A useful minimal replay set is all six no-vision/direct trajectories from the current pilot: both directions for panorama motion, physical torque and intentional turn. Those trajectories were not controlled by either vision estimate. They provide actual gait-induced image changes while retaining both shadow predictions. This is a declared coverage choice, not selection of the best-performing trials. Add the complete remaining trajectory set if the diagnostic warrants it; report every replay included.

The physical pipeline must still compare direct control with the DNa-current adapter and its silencing intervention. A cleaner flow field is insufficient evidence for improved duck behavior.

Before any product integration, add an outer freshness/instability gate. The research `MotionController` sets its observed rate to zero when stale but keeps its integrated angle, which can continue emitting yaw/current. The delivered25Hz simulated frames and deliberate no-vision interventions make this pilot's comparison interpretable; they do not establish safe missing-frame behavior for a live scene. A real adapter must prevent stale retained error from steering, with an explicit reset/reacquisition policy. Preserve simulation-time capture/model clocks separately from webcam wall-clock age; a40ms neural interval is not evidence that a camera image is only40ms old.
