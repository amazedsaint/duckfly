# Spatial Flyvis extraction, version 1

This bounded study validates extraction from the packaged WASM/checkpoint before any motor bridge. It does not establish that Flyvis improves duck control. The implementation keeps every signed raw value and its reference baseline; diagnostic summaries below are derived separately and explicitly.

The eight calibration presentations are bright/dark edges moving at 0, 90, 180 and 270 image degrees. Thirty held-out seeds (1000–1029) use intermediate angles, different contrasts and edge softness, with speed varied independently of direction. Each seed has an edge and its exact direction reversal under both polarities. A further pair of local flashes under each polarity tests spatial location. Blank, uniform flash and uniform flicker movies are controls. This gives 338 trials. No threshold or decoder is fitted on held-out movies.

Inputs are rendered as 96×64 grayscale RGBA pinhole camera frames and sampled with DuckFly's explicit 721-coordinate retina. Capture intervals are 20 ms; the actual packaged 2 ms WASM core takes ten steps per held image. The inherited canonical `shared/vision/stimuli.js` is not changed; this study adds direction/contrast-controlled movies rather than relabeling its fixed edge direction. Initial state is the packaged one-second neutral fade-in checkpoint, common to all independently reset eyes. The input starts at neutral gray. Its 8-bit representation is 128/255, while the reference neutral is 0.5; blank drift is therefore measured rather than assumed exactly zero.

## Gates fixed before the first run

1. **Extraction correctness.** The existing upstream numerical oracle must pass, every retained map sample must equal the indexed full-state value, and delta must equal Float32(raw−baseline). Identity, duplicate coordinates, out-of-range indices, signed values and invalid clocks have direct software tests. Exact checkpoint replay and independent recurrent eye state are required.
2. **Spatial location.** Use the absolute baseline-subtracted activity centroid across the eight maps during the local-flash window [0.1, 0.28] s. The centroid must be in the flash's left/right half of the image in at least 90% of held-out flashes separately for ON and OFF. This is event location, not LC11 object identity or biological gaze control.
3. **Exploratory direction readout.** Integrate positive delta per population in the central 20-degree retinal disk. Calibration presentations give each population a response-weighted preferred vector. Predict held-out directions from that frozen vector-weighted activity. At least 75% must lie in the correct direction hemisphere and median circular error must be ≤60 degrees, separately per polarity. This is a deliberately small decoder diagnostic, not an anatomical HS/VS reconstruction or a claim of control benefit.
4. **Direction confounds.** A direction is considered strong when its vector magnitude reaches the lower quartile of calibration edge magnitudes. No more than 10% of each stationary control family may cross this threshold. Uniform flash and flicker are not expected to leave all neurons silent; this gate concerns the derived direction claim. Failure means the candidate cannot be promoted as a reliable motion detector even if the maps and location gate pass.
5. **Runtime.** Report current per-step, per-20 ms interval and extraction timing distributions, plus reset costs. A single-eye interval p95 ≤20 ms is the measured real-time gate. Two-eye or multi-duck performance remains unmeasured. No physical promotion follows a timing pass.

All trial failures remain in denominators. Summaries report Wilson 95% intervals for proportions. This is a deterministic synthetic engineering screen, not a biological power calculation or held-out physiological validation. Calibration angles are disjoint from held-out angles; the published checkpoint's original training data are unchanged and this study makes no new claim about them.

## Retained evidence

The run stores its exact source and model hashes, every sampled input frame, shared spatial metadata, baseline maps and trial/frame summaries. Full raw/delta spatial snapshots are retained at frame 6, 12 and 23 for every trial as little-endian Float32 binary with indexed offsets. These snapshots supplement exact deterministic inputs and source rather than pretending that every intermediate full-state vector was saved. `replay-spatial.mjs` verifies retained snapshots against a fresh WASM replay. Raw WASM state is copied before any subsequent allocation or step can overwrite it.

The baseline and response maps remain signed. Positive clipping occurs only in the explicitly named exploratory direction summary. No test requests duck motion, changes a circuit current or deploys the application.
